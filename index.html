About me

I work on safe and feasible adaptive control: keeping uncertain machines inside their limits.

I'm a Research Associate in the Formal Control and Autonomous Systems (FOCAS) Lab, Department of Cyber-Physical Systems, IISc Bengaluru, working with Dr. Pushpak Jagtap on safe control and robotic autonomy.

I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at IIT Delhi (2026) under Prof. Shubhendu Bhasin as a Prime Minister's Research Fellow, after a B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering at NIT Durgapur. My work makes controllers respect state, input and input-rate constraints, with feasibility guarantees you can verify ahead of time.

Beyond the equations, I'm behind a camera, catching the stories that happen in quiet moments.

02

Research

Real machines have limits: joints that can't bend too far, motors that can't push too hard. Their models are never exact either. I design adaptive controllers that learn the unknowns while running, never cross those limits, and come with a simple check that says in advance whether the limits can be met at all.

i

Barrier functions

An energy-like function that rises to infinity at a limit, so the controller pushes back harder the closer the system gets to it.

ii

Input saturation

The actuator limit is part of the design, not an afterthought, and the stability proof absorbs the shortfall.

iii

Verifiable feasibility

Closed-form conditions that link state limits, input limits and the reference, so a design is certified before it runs.
